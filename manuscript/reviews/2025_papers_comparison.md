## Three benchmark papers

### [16] Liu et al. 2013 — bulk microarray precursor
- Sci Rep 2013;3:3467 (10 pages)
- 1,091 GBM + 524 AD bulk microarray
- Inverse regulation: ERK/MAPK (GBM up) vs Angiopoietin (AD up)
- Wet-lab: APPswe mouse, Aβ suppresses GBM growth via ERK-AKT-p21

### [17] Liu et al. 2025 — TNFRSF12A candidate-gene TSG scan
- Sci Rep 2025;15:21535 (18 pages), DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-08000-7
- Authors: Ting Liu, Jingjing Pu (Shanghai) + Amit Sharma (Bonn)
- Data: bulk + scRNA-seq (mixed)
- Approach: candidate-driven — 1,217 TSG scan
- Additional: Mendelian randomization + cell-cell communication
- Wet-lab: in vitro TNFRSF12A knockdown → APP processing + Wnt
- Main finding: TNFRSF12A single gene
- Framework: 'overlapping AND oppositely regulated candidates' (mixed direction)
- Biology focus: APP + Wnt + immune

### [18] Xu et al. 2025 — Mitochondrial ML markers
- Front Aging Neurosci 2025;17:1536142 (29 pages), DOI 10.3389/fnagi.2025.1536142
- Authors: Xu et al. — Anhui Medical University
- Data: snRNA-seq (AD) + scRNA-seq (GBM) + DNA methylation (ROSMAP, ADNI, TCGA, CGGA)
- Approach: ML-driven — 10 algorithms marker discovery
- Main finding: 4 mitochondrial markers (EFHD1, SASH1, FAM110B, SLC25A18)
- Additional: oligodendrocyte-astrocyte interactions, APP signaling, HS6ST3, TUBB2B hubs
- Wet-lab: none (methylation validation only)
- Biology focus: mitochondrial + oligodendrocyte
