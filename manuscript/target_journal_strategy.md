## Waterfall submission strategy (hybrid/subscription only)

### Primary target: Briefings in Bioinformatics (Oxford Academic, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~9.5
- **Model:** Hybrid — subscription default, optional OA ~£3,650
- **Author pays:** NO (unless OA chosen)
- **Neden uygun:** Pre-registration + reproducibility disiplini, cross-disease bioinformatics scope
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~40-50% (with major revision)
- **Review süresi:** ~2-3 ay
- **⚠ VERIFY:** hybrid status still valid (Oxford transitions monitored)

### Secondary A: Cancer Letters (Elsevier, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~9.1
- **Model:** Hybrid
- **Neden uygun:** GBM tarafı için güçlü venue; AD cross-disease angle novel
- **Risk:** Orta-yüksek — cancer-focused venue, AD tarafı ikna edici olmalı
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~30-40%

### Secondary B: Neurobiology of Disease (Elsevier, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~5.6
- **Model:** Hybrid
- **Neden uygun:** AD/nörodejenerasyon vurgusu
- **Zayıflık:** GBM tarafı sınır konu olabilir
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~50-60%

### Tertiary: Molecular Neurobiology (Springer, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~5.1
- **Model:** Hybrid
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~50-60%

### Safety net A: Bioinformatics (Oxford, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~5.8
- **Method-focused venue**
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~50-60%

### Safety net B: Neuroinformatics (Springer, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~3.9
- **Method-heavy manuscript'ler için uygun**
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~65-75%

### Ultimate safety: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease (IOS Press, hybrid)

- **IF:** ~3.4
- **AD-focused, GBM tarafı harder sell**
- **Kabul tahmini:** ~70-80%
